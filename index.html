what's up

I'm a graphic and motion designer who likes turning a busy brief into one clear idea. Most of my work is ads, social posts and short motion pieces, and I care about what happens after the scroll stops: does the viewer know what to do next? Lately that's meant QR codes on mall screens and referral codes people actually use.

Tini Express display ad
